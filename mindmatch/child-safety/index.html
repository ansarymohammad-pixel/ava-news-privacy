Safety standards

MindMatch Child Safety Standards

MindMatch, operated by AVA Intelligent, is designed for professional startup networking and has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation.

Open AVA Intelligent
Policy contact support@avaintelligent.info

Use the subject line: Child Safety - MindMatch.

Zero tolerance

Prohibited conduct

MindMatch has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). CSAM, grooming, sextortion, trafficking and sexual exploitation of minors are prohibited on MindMatch.

Users may not upload, share, request, promote, facilitate, or attempt to obtain child sexual abuse material or any sexualized content involving minors.

Reporting

How users can report concerns

Users can report concerning users, messages, posts and images. Reports can be sent to support@avaintelligent.info with the subject line Child Safety - MindMatch.

Please include the relevant username, profile, message, post, image reference, date, and any other information that can help the review.

Review and enforcement

What happens after a report

Reported content will be reviewed and removed when appropriate. Accounts violating this policy may be suspended or deleted.

Confirmed CSAM will be reported to the appropriate authorities and relevant child-safety organizations where required by law.

Scope

Applies to MindMatch and AVA Intelligent

These standards apply to MindMatch and AVA Intelligent services, including profiles, messages, posts, images, user-generated content, and any related community or matching features.

MindMatch may update these standards as the product evolves, especially when new communication, image, profile, or community features are released.
